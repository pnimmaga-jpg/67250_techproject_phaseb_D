Exhibition 1: Pulse Theory: The Architecture of Sound

Pulse Theory explores the structural foundations of electronic music, breaking down how rhythm, tempo, and frequency shape the way we experience sound. Through interactive installations and live demonstrations, visitors can engage with the building blocks of EDM, experimenting with beats and layers to understand how tracks evolve from simple patterns into complex, high-energy compositions.

Exhibition 2: After Hours: The Culture of the Underground

After Hours dives into the history and evolution of underground electronic music scenes, tracing their influence from hidden warehouse parties to global festival stages. Featuring visual media, curated soundscapes, and recreated club environments, the exhibition captures the raw energy and community-driven spirit that continue to define EDM culture today.
